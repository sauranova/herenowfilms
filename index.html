The Founder

Mayur Soneji

Gujarati film actor, storyteller, and founder of HereNow Films Pvt Ltd. Co-founded with Mitali Mayur Soneji, the company has grown into Gujarat's creative powerhouse spanning film, education, advertising, and events.

Known for his roles in Victor 303, Kasoombo, Samandar, and Saiyar Mori Re, Mayur brings the same cinematic intensity to every project HereNow touches.
