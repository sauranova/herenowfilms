Welcome to HERENOW

A World Built on Story, Purpose & the Power of Now

HERENOW Films Pvt. Ltd. is not just a company. It is a creative ecosystem — alive, purposeful, and rooted in the belief that every human being carries a story the world is waiting to hear. Founded in 2018 by actor Mayur Soneji and co-founded with Mitali Mayur Soneji, it was born in Rajkot, in the heart of Saurashtra.

The most powerful place any human being can ever stand is here — fully present, fully alive, fully committed to this moment. Not yesterday's regret. Not tomorrow's fear. Here. Now. That philosophy sits at the heart of everything we do.

From feature films like Kasoombo and Victor 303 to brand campaigns like Vasundhara, we stand on four distinct pillars — meeting every student, brand, storyteller and dreamer exactly where they are.

In My Own Words

The Founder's Letter

— Mayur Soneji, Founder

I did not choose this path. This path chose me.

From the time I was a young boy walking the disciplined corridors of Sainik School, Balachadi, I knew that something inside me was searching — not just for a career, but for a calling. That search led me to the study of the human mind through Psychology at M.S. University, Baroda, then deeper into the art of guiding others through my Bachelor of Education from IGNOU. Every step, every classroom, every stage I stood upon was quietly preparing me for something larger than I could then imagine.

I have lived many lives as an actor. I have breathed through the frames of Shoorveer on JioHotstar, walked the emotional landscapes of Vitthal Teedi, Saiyar Mori Re, Kasoombo, Samandar, Victor 303, and many more. Each role taught me something no textbook ever could — that storytelling is not a profession. It is a responsibility. A responsibility to truth. To the audience. To the human experience in all its complexity and beauty.

But a moment came — still and certain as dawn — when I realised that performing alone was not enough. That my purpose was not just to tell stories, but to build a world where stories could be born, shaped, and shared at every level of the creative journey. That moment became HERENOW Films Pvt. Ltd.

Why HERENOW?

Because the most powerful place any human being can ever stand is here — fully present, fully alive, fully committed to this moment. Not yesterday's regret. Not tomorrow's fear. Here. Now.

That is the philosophy at the heart of everything we do. Whether you are a student stepping onto a stage for the first time, a brand searching for its voice, a storyteller with a vision that demands to be filmed, or a dreamer wanting to create an event the world will not forget — we meet you exactly where you are. And together, we bring out what was always within you.

A Message From My Heart

I started HERENOW Films Pvt. Ltd. with a simple, profound belief — that creativity, when practiced with purpose, has the power to transform lives. Not just the lives of artists and performers, but the life of every person who watches a film that moves them, every customer who encounters a brand that truly speaks to them, every guest who attends an event that makes them feel something real, and every student who walks out of a classroom knowing — for the first time — exactly who they are.

Rajkot is my home. Saurashtra is my soil. And it is here — in this city, in this culture, in this deeply rooted and beautifully alive part of Gujarat — that I chose to plant this seed. Because I believe the stories of this land deserve to be told with the same craft, the same scale, and the same reverence as any story told anywhere in the world.

To every dreamer, every creator, every brand, every storyteller who finds their way to us — welcome. You are exactly where you are meant to be. Here. Now.

— Mayur Soneji, Founder, HERENOW Films Pvt. Ltd.
